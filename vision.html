THE ROAD AHEAD

What's next for NEXORA.

We are building carefully, not rushing to label unfinished ideas as features. These are directions we hope to explore as NEXORA grows.

Future direction

A personal account space

A clearer profile and account centre, with account access that stays easy to find and manage.

Future direction

Optional sign-in

Explore Google, Apple, phone and email sign-in while keeping guest access available as an entry point.

Core principle

Respect for existing progress

When accounts arrive, guest data should have a safe, understandable path into an account — never a silent overwrite.

Long-term vision

Community and sharing

Consider thoughtful ways for people to connect, share progress and learn from one another, with meaningful controls.

Long-term vision

NOVA AI

A future AI companion concept intended to make planning and reflection feel more personal and useful.

Long-term vision

A wider NEXORA ecosystem

As the project develops, explore the right structure, partnerships and experiences to support its next stage.

This page describes development intentions, not delivery commitments. Features, priorities and timing may change as the project evolves.
