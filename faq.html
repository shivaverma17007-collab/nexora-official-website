GOOD TO KNOW

A few things you might wonder.

Clear answers about what NEXORA is, what you can use today and what is still being imagined for tomorrow.

What is NEXORA?

NEXORA is a productivity and self-development app with tools for tasks, study, routines, trackers, journaling, mindful breathing and more.

Who is NEXORA for?

For anyone who wants a little more structure and space for personal progress — whether you're studying, organising everyday tasks or building routines.

Do I need an account to explore NEXORA?

The product direction is guest-first, with optional sign-in planned. Account and sign-in capabilities are still being developed; check the app itself for the currently available access options.

Are all the ideas on the Vision page available now?

No. The Vision page separates future concepts — such as profiles, optional sign-in, community and NOVA AI — from the tools currently described as part of the app.

Does NEXORA expect me to be productive every minute?

Not at all. The idea is progress without unnecessary pressure. Use what helps, take breaks and make the experience your own.

Where can I share feedback?

Write to shivaverma17007@gmail.com. Questions, suggestions and thoughtful feedback are welcome.

Where can I read about privacy and terms?

Visit the Privacy Policy and Terms pages. Both remain marked as drafts until the app's actual data practices and legal details have been verified.
